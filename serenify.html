Breathing Exercises

A guided breathing tool helps users manage anxiety, stress, and panic attacks. It offers various techniques such as box breathing and deep relaxation exercises, complete with soothing visuals and sounds to calm the mind.

Box Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4.

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8.

Wellness Tips & Guidance

The app provides daily tips for improving sleep quality, preventing stress, and maintaining emotional well-being. Each tip is practical, science-backed, and personalized to the user's needs and mood patterns.

  • Mindfulness Practice: Spend 5 minutes daily focusing on your breath to reduce stress.
  • Regular Exercise: Engage in physical activity like walking or yoga to boost your mood and energy levels.
  • Sleep Hygiene: Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep by maintaining a consistent bedtime routine.
  • Healthy Eating: Incorporate balanced meals with fruits, vegetables, and whole grains to support brain health.
  • Social Connections: Reach out to friends or family for support and maintain meaningful relationships.
